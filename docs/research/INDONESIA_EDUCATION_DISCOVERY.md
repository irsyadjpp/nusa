## 1. Major Educational Domains

### 1.1 Educational Levels (Jenjang Pendidikan)

**Early Childhood Education (PAUD)**
- Target: Children from birth to 6 years
- Purpose: Developmental stimulation to prepare for further education
- Structure: Fase Fondasi (Foundation Phase)
- Standards: Based on child development achievement standards

**Basic Education (Pendidikan Dasar)**
- Primary: SD/MI (Sekolah Dasar/Madrasah Ibtidaiyah) - Grades 1-6
- Junior Secondary: SMP/MTs (Sekolah Menengah Pertama/Madrasah Tsanawiyah) - Grades 7-9
- Equivalency: Paket A (primary), Paket B (junior secondary)
- Structure: Fase A (Grades 1-2), Fase B (Grades 3-4), Fase C (Grades 5-6), Fase D (Grades 7-9)

**Secondary Education (Pendidikan Menengah)**
- Senior General: SMA/MA (Sekolah Menengah Atas/Madrasah Aliyah) - Grades 10-12
- Vocational: SMK/MAK (Sekolah Menengah Kejuruan/Madrasah Aliyah Kejuruan) - Grades 10-12/13
- Equivalency: Paket C (senior secondary)
- Structure: Fase E (Grade 10), Fase F (Grades 11-12/13)

**Special Education (Pendidikan Khusus)**
- SLB (Sekolah Luar Biasa) for students with special needs
- Inclusive education in regular schools
- Program Pendidikan Individual (PPI) for personalized learning plans

**Non-formal Education**
- PKBM (Pusat Kegiatan Belajar Masyarakat)
- SKB (Sanggar Kegiatan Belajar)
- Homeschooling programs

### 1.2 Educational Pathways (Jalur Pendidikan)
- Formal education (regular schools)
- Non-formal education (community learning centers)
- Informal education (family and community-based learning)

## 2. Educational Concepts

### 2.1 Core Concepts

**Kurikulum Merdeka (Independent Curriculum)**
- Student-centered curriculum emphasizing character development
- Reduced content load to allow for deep learning
- Flexibility for schools to adapt to local context
- Implemented in over 330,000 schools
- Shows positive impact on literacy and numeracy scores based on National Assessment data

**Profil Pelajar Pancasila (Pancasila Student Profile)**
- Vision: "Pelajar Indonesia merupakan pelajar sepanjang hayat yang kompeten, berkarakter, dan berperilaku sesuai nilai-nilai Pancasila"
- Six dimensions:
  1. Beriman, bertakwa kepada Tuhan Yang Maha Esa, dan berakhlak mulia
  2. Berkebinekaan global
  3. Bergotong-royong
  4. Mandiri
  5. Bernalar kritis
  6. Kreatif
- Serves as the ultimate goal of national education
- Reference for designing National Education Standards (SNP)

**Pembelajaran Mendalam (Deep Learning)**
- Approach to address learning crisis
- Framework with four components:
  1. Graduate profile dimensions (8 dimensions)
  2. Learning principles (mindful, meaningful, joyful)
  3. Learning experiences (understanding, applying, reflecting)
  4. Learning framework (pedagogical practices, learning environment, learning partnerships, digital technology)
- Focuses on higher-order thinking skills
- Aims for meaningful and joyful learning

**Capaian Pembelajaran (Learning Outcomes - CP)**
- Defined for each educational phase
- Phased benchmarks for student achievement
- Foundation for curriculum development
- Regulated by Keputusan Kepala BSKAP No. 046/H/KR/2025

**Kemampuan Fondasi (Foundation Abilities)**
- Six abilities for children transitioning from PAUD to SD:
  1. Mengenal Nilai Agama dan Budi Pekerti
  2. Kematangan Emosi yang Cukup untuk Berkegiatan di Lingkungan Belajar
  3. Keterampilan Sosial dan Bahasa yang Memadai untuk Berinteraksi Sehat
  4. Pemaknaan Terhadap Belajar yang Positif
  5. Pengembangan Keterampilan Motorik dan Perawatan Diri
  6. Kematangan Kognitif untuk Melakukan Kegiatan Belajar
- Bridge between PAUD and early basic education
- Not CP or KD, but values, knowledge, and skills to be built

### 2.2 Assessment Concepts

**Formative Assessment**
- Integrated with learning process
- Oriented toward student competence development
- Continuous cycle of assessment
- Emphasizes authentic and holistic evaluation

**Summative Assessment**
- Evaluates competency achievement
- Used for promotion and graduation decisions
- Comprehensive evaluation of learning outcomes

**National Assessment (AN)**
- Replaces traditional national examinations
- Focuses on literacy, numeracy, and character
- Includes Rapor Pendidikan (Education Report)
- Data-driven approach to education quality improvement

## 3. Curriculum Structures

### 3.1 Phase-Based Structure (Struktur Berbasis Fase)

**Fase Fondasi (Foundation Phase)**
- PAUD level
- Focus on developmental stimulation
- Six foundation abilities

**Fase A (Grades 1-2)**
- Early primary education
- Emphasis on basic literacy and numeracy
- Transition from foundation abilities

**Fase B (Grades 3-4)**
- Mid-primary education
- Deepening basic skills
- Introduction to subject-based learning

**Fase C (Grades 5-6)**
- Upper primary education
- Preparation for secondary education
- More complex learning activities

**Fase D (Grades 7-9)**
- Junior secondary education
- Subject specialization begins
- Preparation for senior secondary or vocational track

**Fase E (Grade 10)**
- Senior secondary first year
- Track selection (general or vocational)
- Foundation for specialized learning

**Fase F (Grades 11-12/13)**
- Senior secondary final years
- Specialized learning
- Preparation for higher education or workforce

### 3.2 Curriculum Components

**Intrakurikuler (Intracurricular)**
- Regular classroom instruction
- Subject-based learning
- Core curriculum implementation

**Kokurikuler (Co-curricular)**
- Activities to strengthen, deepen, and enrich intracurricular learning
- Flexible in content, activities, and timing
- Character and competency development
- Can involve parents, community, and industry partners

**Ekstrakurikuler (Extracurricular)**
- Optional activities outside regular curriculum
- Student interest-based
- Talent development
- Social and leadership skills

**Projek Profil Pelajar Pancasila (P5)**
- Project-based learning to develop Pancasila Student Profile
- Integrates multiple subjects
- Real-world problem solving
- Character development focus

### 3.3 Curriculum Development

**Kurikulum Satuan Pendidikan (School-Level Curriculum)**
- Developed by each educational unit
- Adapted to local context and student needs
- Based on national standards (CP)
- For SMK: Implementative curriculum that elaborates core curriculum into concentrations

**Principles of Curriculum Development**
- Student-centered
- Contextual and relevant
- Flexible and adaptive
- Character-oriented
- Competency-based

## 4. Learning Structures

### 4.1 Deep Learning Framework

**Four Components:**

1. **Graduate Profile Dimensions (8 dimensions)**
   - Faith and piety
   - Citizenship
   - Critical reasoning
   - Creativity
   - Collaboration
   - Independence
   - Health
   - Communication

2. **Learning Principles**
   - Mindful (sadar)
   - Meaningful (bermakna)
   - Joyful (menyenangkan)

3. **Learning Experiences**
   - Understanding (memahami)
   - Applying (menerapkan)
   - Reflecting (merefleksikan)

4. **Learning Framework**
   - Pedagogical practices
   - Learning environment
   - Learning partnerships
   - Digital technology utilization

### 4.2 Learning Approaches

**STEM Education (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics)**
- Interdisciplinary approach
- Scientific method application in daily contexts
- Encourages critical thinking, creativity, innovation, collaboration
- Implemented through intracurricular, co-curricular, or extracurricular
- Examples provided for PAUD, SD, SMP, SMA, SMK, inclusive, and SLB

**Coding and Artificial Intelligence Education**
- Urgency in Industry 4.0 and Society 5.0 era
- Focus on computational thinking, digital literacy, algorithmic programming
- Ethical responsibility in technology use
- Elective subject from elementary to high school
- Requires teacher training and certification

**Financial Literacy Education**
- Elements: earning income, managing budget, saving, managing risk
- Principles: PINTAR (Pancasila, Ilmu Pengetahuan dan Praktik Nyata, Tepat Sasaran, Adaptif dan Kontekstual, Rinci dan Komprehensif)
- Implementation through intracurricular, co-curricular, extracurricular
- Collaboration with financial institutions

**Anti-Corruption Education**
- Values: honesty, discipline, responsibility, hard work, justice, care, courage
- Not a separate subject but integrated contextually
- Implementation through various educational activities
- Collaboration with KPK (Corruption Eradication Commission)

**Anti-Drugs Education (IKAN - Integrasi Kurikulum Anti Narkoba)**
- Strategic role of education in drug prevention
- Integrated into intracurricular, co-curricular, extracurricular, and school culture
- Collaboration with BNN (National Narcotics Agency)
- Focus on building awareness, resilience, and healthy decision-making

**Climate Change Education**
- Environmental awareness
- Sustainable development
- Climate action
- Integrated across subjects

### 4.3 Special Education Learning

**Program Pendidikan Individual (PPI)**
- Individualized education program for students with special needs
- Designed by educators specifically for each student
- Addresses academic and non-academic difficulties
- Components: assessment, goals, strategies, evaluation
- Used in special schools and inclusive education settings

## 6. Educational Workflows

### 6.1 Curriculum Development Workflow

**National Level**
1. Research and policy development
2. CP formulation (BSKAP)
3. Standards development
4. Guidelines preparation
5. Implementation support

**School Level**
1. Context analysis
2. Vision, mission, goals formulation
3. Curriculum organization
4. Learning planning
5. Implementation
6. Evaluation and revision

### 6.2 Learning Planning Workflow

**CP → TP → ATP Structure**
- CP (Capaian Pembelajaran): Learning outcomes defined nationally
- TP (Tujuan Pembelajaran): Learning objectives formulated by teachers
- ATP (Alur Tujuan Pembelajaran): Learning activity sequences designed by teachers

**Alternative Workflows**
- Not limited to CP → TP → ATP
- Flexible based on school context
- Can be adapted for special education (PPI)
- Project-based learning pathways

### 6.3 Assessment Workflow

**Formative Cycle**
1. Learning activity
2. Observation and documentation
3. Feedback provision
4. Learning adjustment
5. Continuous improvement

**Summative Cycle**
1. Comprehensive assessment
2. Result processing
3. Reporting
4. Promotion/graduation decision
5. Follow-up actions

### 6.4 Quality Assurance Workflow

**SPMI (Sistem Penjaminan Mutu Internal)**
- Mapping quality conditions
- Planning improvement strategies
- Implementation
- Evaluation
- Control and monitoring
- Cycle repeats continuously

**SPME (Sistem Penjaminan Mutu Eksternal)**
- Accreditation
- External evaluation
- Independent assessment
- Quality certification

## 10. Recent Changes

### 10.1 Ministry Restructuring (2024)
- Establishment of Kementerian Pendidikan Dasar dan Menengah (Kemendikdasmen)
- Separation from previous Kemendikbudristek
- Perpres No. 188 Tahun 2024
- Focus on basic and secondary education

### 10.2 Curriculum Evolution

**From Kurikulum 2013 to Kurikulum Merdeka**
- Emergency Curriculum during pandemic
- Kurikulum Merdeka as recovery curriculum
- Gradual implementation
- Over 330,000 schools implementing Kurikulum Merdeka

**Key Changes:**
- More flexible and student-centered
- Reduced content load
- Emphasis on character development
- Phase-based structure instead of grade-based
- Project-based learning integration

### 10.3 Assessment Reform

**From National Examinations to National Assessment**
- Shift from high-stakes testing to diagnostic assessment
- Focus on literacy, numeracy, and character
- Rapor Pendidikan as feedback mechanism
- Data-driven improvement approach

### 10.4 Quality Assurance Evolution

**SPMI Strengthening**
- Academic paper on SPMI published (December 2025)
- Emphasis on internal quality culture
- Dashboard monitoring system development
- Integration with Rapor Pendidikan

### 10.5 Regulatory Updates

**Standar Isi Update (2025)**
- Permendikdasmen No. 12 Tahun 2025 replaces Permendikbudristek No. 8 Tahun 2024
- Adaptation to legal and developmental needs
- Alignment with PP No. 57 Tahun 2021 and PP No. 4 Tahun 2022

## Conclusion

The Indonesian education ecosystem is undergoing significant transformation with Kurikulum Merdeka at its core. The system emphasizes:

1. **Student-centered learning** with flexibility for local adaptation
2. **Character development** through Profil Pelajar Pancasila as the ultimate goal
3. **Deep learning** approaches that are mindful, meaningful, and joyful
4. **Comprehensive assessment** that goes beyond academic achievement
5. **Quality assurance** through both internal and external mechanisms
6. **Multi-stakeholder collaboration** involving government, community, and industry partners
7. **Digital transformation** with AI and technology integration
8. **Inclusive education** that addresses diverse learning needs
9. **Future skills development** including STEM, coding, financial literacy, and anti-corruption education
10. **Continuous improvement** through data-driven decision making

The ecosystem is supported by a robust regulatory framework, clear hierarchical relationships, and strong collaborative networks. Recent changes include ministry restructuring, curriculum reform, assessment transformation, and quality assurance strengthening. Emerging trends focus on digital transformation, character education, life skills, inclusive education, and future skills preparation.

Future directions point toward continued curriculum refinement, assessment enhancement, teacher development, technology integration, multi-stakeholder collaboration, and research-based innovation. The system aims to produce lifelong learners who are competent, character-driven, and behave according to Pancasila values, preparing them for both national and global citizenship in the 21st century.
